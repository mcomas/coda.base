# Chemical composition of volcanic rocks from Kilauea Iki

The \`kilauea_iki\` data set contains the chemical composition of
volcanic rocks sampled from the lava lake at Kilauea Iki (Hawaii). The
data represent major oxide concentrations in fractional form.

## Usage

``` r
kilauea_iki
```

## Format

A data frame with 17 observations and 11 variables:

- SiO2:

  Silicon dioxide

- TiO2:

  Titanium dioxide

- Al2O3:

  Aluminium oxide

- Fe2O3:

  Ferric oxide

- FeO:

  Ferrous oxide

- MnO:

  Manganese oxide

- MgO:

  Magnesium oxide

- CaO:

  Calcium oxide

- Na2O:

  Sodium oxide

- K2O:

  Potassium oxide

- P2O5:

  Phosphorus pentoxide

## Source

Richter, D. H., & Moore, J. G. (1966). *Petrology of Kilauea Iki lava
lake, Hawaii*. Geological Survey Professional Paper 537-B.

## Details

The variability in oxide concentrations is attributed to magnesian
olivine fractionation from a single magmatic mass, as suggested by
Richter and Moore (1966).
