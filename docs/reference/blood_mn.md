# The MN blood system

In humans, the main blood group systems are the ABO system, the Rh
system, and the MN system. The MN blood system is related to proteins of
the red blood cell plasma membrane. Its inheritance pattern is autosomal
with codominance, meaning that the heterozygous phenotype is distinct
from both homozygous phenotypes.

The three phenotypes are M, N, and MN. Their frequencies vary across
populations. Under the Hardy-Weinberg principle, allele and genotype
frequencies remain constant across generations in the absence of
evolutionary forces, implying that

\\\frac{x\_{MM} x\_{NN}}{x\_{MN}^2} = \frac{1}{4}\\

where \\x\_{MM}\\ and \\x\_{NN}\\ are the genotype frequencies of the
homozygotes and \\x\_{MN}\\ is the genotype frequency of heterozygotes.

## Usage

``` r
blood_mn
```

## Format

An object of class `data.frame` with 49 rows and 5 columns.
