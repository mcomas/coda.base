# Household budget patterns

In a sample survey of single persons living alone in rented
accommodation, twenty men and twenty women were randomly selected and
asked to record their expenditure over one month in the following four
mutually exclusive and exhaustive commodity groups:

- \`Hous\`: housing, including fuel and light,

- \`Food\`: foodstuffs, including alcohol and tobacco,

- \`Serv\`: services, including transport and vehicles,

- \`Other\`: other goods, including clothing, footwear, and durable
  goods.

## Usage

``` r
household_budget
```

## Format

An object of class `data.frame` with 40 rows and 6 columns.
